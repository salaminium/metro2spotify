Before you start

Track matching uses Spotify search, so ambiguous titles may match a different version. The app processes tracks one by one to reduce rate-limit errors. For 1,650 tracks, keep this page open until it finishes. Spotify may impose API limits or account restrictions.

This is a browser-based personal tool, not affiliated with Spotify or Metrolist. It does not upload your CSV to a separate server; search queries are sent to Spotify's API.
